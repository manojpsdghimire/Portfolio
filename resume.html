ENTERPRISE SYSTEMS

Making disconnected systems work together

Connecting workforce applications, business processes, and enterprise data into cohesive experiences.

The challenge

Information and workflows often span disconnected systems and organizational teams.

My contribution

Application architecture, integrations, data transformation, and full-stack development.

What matters

Reliable information flows, maintainability, scalability, and intuitive user experiences.

🔐
SECURITY & GOVERNANCE

Security shouldn't be an afterthought

Designing applications where identity, authorization, data protection, and auditability are foundational.

The challenge

Enterprise environments require precise access boundaries and auditable administration.

My contribution

SSO, role-based authorization, access governance, audit logging, and security documentation.

What matters

Least privilege, traceability, and secure-by-design implementation.

⚙️
WORKFLOW AUTOMATION

Turning complicated rules into simple experiences

Building configurable processes for assignments, approvals, performance workflows, and tracking.

The challenge

Policies change, responsibilities vary, and manual coordination creates complexity.

My contribution

Workflow engines, assignment rules, state models, audit history, and reporting.

What matters

Predictability, transparency, and flexibility.

✨
AI & INNOVATION

Finding useful places for AI

Developing and exploring AI-assisted experiences that help people work with information more effectively.

The challenge

Useful organizational information can be difficult to find and act upon.

My contribution

Azure OpenAI integration, contextual workflows, and sensitive-data protection considerations.

What matters

Practical value, responsible AI, and human oversight.
